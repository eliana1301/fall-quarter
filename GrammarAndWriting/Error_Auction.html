Winning team: Bid: $100
Your team: Is it correct? If not, fix it. Then say why.

Did the team get it right?
Approach International Student CenterGrammar and Writing · Independent Level

How to Play

  1. Work in six teams. Every team starts with $1,000.
  2. Read the sentence with your team. Is it correct or not?
  3. Bid for the sentence. The lowest bid is $100. You cannot bid more money than you have.
  4. The highest bid buys it. Say if it is correct. If not, fix it and say why.
  5. Right answer: you win your bid. Wrong answer: you lose your bid.
  6. Some lots have a surprise. The team with the most money at the end wins.
