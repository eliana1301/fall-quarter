Grammar in Context  |  Week 4  |  Present Perfect, Simple Past, Past Perfect

I am working

1Before You Start HOW IT WORKS

  1. Interview your partner. Then change roles. About 15 minutes each.
  2. Choose at least 8 questions. Question 12 is for everyone.
  3. Always ask the follow-up question.
  4. Write short notes, not full sentences. You will use them to tell your group about your partner.

The colored labels show the tense the question uses: Present perfect Simple past Past perfect Free answer

2Life in the U.S. INTERVIEW

1 Present perfect

How long have you lived in the U.S.?

Follow-up: When did you arrive, and what was your first week like?

2 Present perfect

What is the most surprising thing that has happened to you here?

Follow-up: What happened exactly?

3 Past perfect

Had you ever visited the U.S. before you moved here?

Follow-up: What did you imagine it would be like?

4 Present perfect

Which places have you visited since you arrived?

Follow-up: Which one did you like most, and why?

3Work and Study INTERVIEW

5 Simple past

What did you do for work or study before you came to the U.S.?

Follow-up: How long had you done that before you left?

6 Present perfect

What have you done here to grow professionally?

Follow-up: What has been the biggest difference from your country?

7 Present perfect

What is the biggest professional challenge you have faced?

Follow-up: What did you do about it?

8 Present perfect

What is the best decision you have ever made for your career?

Follow-up: When did you make it, and why?

4Personal Stories INTERVIEW

9 Present perfect

What have you learned to do here that you could not do before?

Follow-up: Who taught you, or how did you learn?

10 Simple pastPast perfect

Tell me about a day here when everything went wrong.

Follow-up: What had happened before that?

11 Present perfect

Have you made any good friends here?

Follow-up: How did you meet them?

5Looking Ahead INTERVIEW

12 Free answer

Where do you see yourself in 3 to 5 years?

Follow-up: What is the first step to get there?

6Meet My Partner GROUPS OF 4

Groups of 4. Share at least 5 facts about your partner (about 2 to 3 minutes). Your partner can add details. Listen and ask one question about each person.

Sentence frames to help you
  • ___ has lived in ___ for / since ___.
  • Before she / he came to the U.S., she / he had ___.
  • She / He has never ___, but she / he wants to.
  • Last year (In 2023, When she / he arrived...), she / he ___.
  • The most surprising thing I have learned about her / him is that ___.
  • In 3 to 5 years, she / he sees herself / himself ___.
Before you talk, check:

Listen: what did you learn about the others?
