2The Day the Nor'easter Hit READING

Read for the story first.

Read: The Day the Nor'easter Hit

Leila is from Casablanca, Morocco. She works at a hotel in Back Bay, Boston.

Last February, I learned what a nor'easter is. I had heard the word on the news, but I had never seen one.

That morning, my alarm didn't ring. My phone had died during the night because I had forgotten to charge it. When I finally woke up, it was 7:45, and I had to start work at the hotel at 8:00.

I looked out the window. Everything was white. About a foot of snow had fallen overnight, and it was still coming down. I got dressed in two minutes and ran out the door.

By the time I reached the bus stop, the bus had already left. I waited for twenty minutes, but no other bus came. Later I found out that the MBTA had cancelled the other morning buses on my route early that morning.

So I walked to the T (Boston's subway). When I got to the station, a long line of people was waiting outside. The trains were running, but very slowly. I finally arrived at the hotel at 9:30. My manager looked at me, and I was sure I was in trouble.

But she smiled. "Don't worry," she said. "Most of the staff hasn't arrived yet. You're one of the first!"

That night, I called my mom in Casablanca. She asked, "Was it cold?" I laughed. I had never felt so cold in my life. But I had also never felt so proud of myself. I had made it to work in a real New England storm.

Now, every time my weather app shows a snowflake, I charge my phone before bed.

4Sentence-Combining Race PRACTICE

Join the two sentences with the word in parentheses. Think: which happened first?

Model answers (open after the race)
  1. By the time I got to the airport, my flight had (already) left.
  2. She couldn't open the door because she had lost her keys.
  3. When we arrived at the restaurant, they had (already) run out of clam chowder.
  4. I had never seen snow before I moved to Boston. (Also correct: I never saw snow before I moved to Boston.)
  5. By the time the delivery driver found our apartment, the pizza had (already) gotten cold.
  6. When I logged on to Zoom, the meeting had (already) ended.
  7. After she had read the reviews, she booked the hotel. (Also correct: After she read the reviews...)
  8. By the time we found our seats at Fenway, the game had (already) started.

5First or Second? QUICK CHECK

Which action happened FIRST? Tap your answer, then check.

1 When I got home, my roommate had cooked dinner.
Sentence 1: what happened first?
Had cooked: dinner was ready before I got home.
2 When I got home, my roommate cooked dinner.
Sentence 2: what happened first?
No had: I got home, then my roommate cooked.
3 The movie had started by the time we sat down.
Sentence 3: what happened first?
Had started: the movie started before we sat down.
4 I didn't go to dinner because I had already eaten.
Sentence 4: what happened first?
Had eaten: I ate before the dinner.
5 After the guests left, we cleaned the kitchen.
Sentence 5: what happened first?
After shows the order: the guests left first.
6 By the time the snowplow came, we had shoveled the driveway.
Sentence 6: what happened first?
Had shoveled: we finished before the snowplow came.
7 I opened the window because the room had gotten too hot.
Sentence 7: what happened first?
Had gotten: the room got hot first.
8 When the teacher came in, the students stopped talking.
Sentence 8: what happened first?
No had: the teacher came in, then the students stopped.

8Sorry, I'm Late! ROLE-PLAY

Groups of 3: A explains what happened, B asks questions, the Coach listens. Change roles after each card.

Situation cards (6)
1. Employee and Manager

A: You missed an important Zoom meeting this morning. Explain what happened.
Ideas: your Wi-Fi / the laptop battery / the wrong link

B: What happened? Had you checked the link before the meeting?

2. Tenant and Landlord

A: You paid the rent five days late. Explain what happened.
Ideas: your bank / a new job / you forgot the date

B: Why was it late? Have you paid on time before?

3. Student and Teacher

A: You didn't send your homework. Explain what happened.
Ideas: your laptop / the email / a family visit

B: What happened? Had you started it?

4. Friend 1 and Friend 2

A: You arrived 45 minutes late to your friend's birthday dinner in the North End. Explain what happened.
Ideas: the T / parking / the wrong restaurant

B: Where were you? Had you left home on time?

5. Hotel guest and Receptionist

A: You arrived at your hotel at 2 a.m., and your room was not available. Explain what happened.
Ideas: a delayed flight / a lost bag / a long taxi line

B: We held the room until midnight, and then we gave it to another guest. Why were you so late?

6. Passenger and Airline agent

A: You missed your connecting flight. Explain what happened.
Ideas: a late first flight / security / the wrong gate

B: What happened? Had you checked the gate number?
