How it works

Setup (5 minutes)

  • Teams of 4 or 5. Pick a team name (click a name at the bottom to change it).
  • Choose a spokesperson for round 1. The spokesperson changes every round.

Category rounds (3 minutes each)

  • The teacher reveals a category.
  • Your team has 3 minutes to poll each other, find your best answer, and prepare a 30-second statement using the present perfect.
  • The spokesperson presents. The class votes. The winning team earns a point.

Grand finale (5 minutes)

  • Each team pitches one final statement: the one thing that makes your team unbeatable.
  • The class picks the champion. The finale is worth 2 points.

Rules

  • Every statement must use the present perfect.
  • Every team member has to help in at least one round.
  • Stories must be true. No exaggeration.
  • The spokesperson changes every round so everyone speaks.
  • 30 seconds means 30 seconds. The timer decides.

Sentence bank

Steal these patterns. Use at least one of each type in every round.

EXPERIENCES · things that happened at some point in your life

  • Our team has been to 27 countries in total.
  • Between us, we have visited every continent except Antarctica.
  • Beatriz has met her favorite actress at Boston Logan Airport.
  • Rafael has climbed a real volcano in Guatemala.
  • Ana has never eaten meat in her whole life.

RECENT EVENTS · today, this week, this month

  • Our team has already visited five different states this year.
  • Ana has just returned from a road trip through New England.
  • Pedro has recently learned to cook three new dishes.
  • We have finished a whole bottle of hot sauce this week.
  • Marcelo has just tried lobster for the first time.

UNFINISHED SITUATIONS · started in the past, still true now

  • Marcelo has been playing guitar for 15 years.
  • Beatriz has known her best friend since kindergarten.
  • Our team has been living in the US for a combined total of 12 years.
  • Ana has been avoiding the dentist for four years.
  • We have been friends since the first week of class.
