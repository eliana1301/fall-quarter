1Find Someone Who SPEAKING

Stand up and talk to as many classmates as you can. Online: talk in your breakout room, then in a new room halfway through.

  1. Write the question first, then ask it. Start with a question like Have you ever ... ?, Did you ... ?, Had you ... ?, Are you ... ?, or Do you ... ?
  2. When someone says yes, write their name and ask one follow-up question (When? Why? How long? What was it like?).
  3. You can use each classmate's name only twice.
